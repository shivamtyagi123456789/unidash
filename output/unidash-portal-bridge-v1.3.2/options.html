UniDash Portal Bridge – Portal settings

Use your MITS-DU institute account on the college portals where supported. UniDash login does not automatically sign you in to Moodle or AMS. The extension will ask permission for each portal site and never asks for or stores your portal password.

Moodle (LMS)
AMS / ERP portal

The MITS defaults scan the dashboard quiz/schedule panels, academic calendar and per-course attendance at /student/dashboard, /student/academic-calendar and /student/courses. You can add more same-site pages, e.g.
[{"name":"Attendance","path":"/Student/Attendance","tableSelector":"table","keyColumns":[0]},
 {"name":"Results","path":"/Student/Result","tableSelector":"table.result","keyColumns":[0,1]}]
Tip: sign in to AMS, open each page you care about, copy the address bar path. keyColumns are the column numbers (from 0) that uniquely identify a row, e.g. subject code.
