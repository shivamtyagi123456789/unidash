    Connect your college portals?

    Any portal that still needs setup will open in a new tab. Sign in there, then return to UniDash.

    Signing in alone does not let UniDash scan portal records. Moodle needs an approved token or calendar feed, and AMS needs a supported read-only access method. UniDash will not collect your portal passwords.
